## Brand & Style

This design system embodies Scandinavian warmth, tactile luxury, and calm authority. Designed for an elevated contemporary fashion house, the interface operates as an understated editorial gallery where garments, fabrics, and silhouettes take absolute precedence over interface decoration.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Warm Architectural Minimalism:** Clean lines governed by generous negative space, natural linen tones, and soft ecru foundations instead of stark clinical whites.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-fashion publication rhythm pairing graceful serif display headings with modern, neutral grotesque typefaces for cataloging, product specs, and transactional utilities.
- **Restrained Tactility:** Visual weight is communicated through fine hairline dividers, tonal contrasts, and deliberate micro-interactions rather than heavy shadows or ornamental framing.
- **Emotional Intent:** The interface should evoke serenity, curated permanence, and effortless discernment.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from raw architectural materials: unbleached cotton, washed limestone, natural wool, and deep carbon dye.

### Roles and Application
- **Canvas Base (`#F9F8F5`):** A warm ecru foundation that prevents eye strain, creates an organic, paper-like ground, and brings warmth to imagery.
- **Primary Ink (`#191817`):** An ultra-dense charcoal providing supreme legibility while feeling softer and more intentional than pure digital black (`#000000`). Used for primary headings, essential microcopy, active indicators, and high-emphasis buttons.
- **Secondary Accent (`#8C7A6B`):** Warm raw umber used for secondary actions, subtle badge backgrounds, and muted category groupings.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#C2B29F`):** Muted oat tone applied to delicate structural lines, disabled states, and hover surface fills.
- **Subtle Surface (`#F2EFEB`):** A stepped tone used for secondary product cards, filter drawers, dropdown panels, and table row shading.
- **Editorial Contrast Surfaces:** Selected hero placements and editorial campaign inserts may invert to deep carbon surfaces (`#191817`) paired with ecru typography (`#F9F8F5`) to punctuate collection releases.

## Typography

The typography system pairs the literary cadence of EB Garamond with the structured clarity of Hanken Grotesk.

- **EB Garamond:** Reserved for editorial headlines, campaign storytelling, seasonal manifestos, and prominent product category titles. It introduces bespoke craftsmanship and heritage.
- **Hanken Grotesk:** Deployed for all operational, navigational, metadata, and transactional data points. Its low-contrast geometry guarantees instant legibility in e-commerce facets, checkout funnels, and size charts.
- **Case Rhythms:** Product tags, category navigation flags, and button labels employ `label-uppercase` tracking (`0.12em`) to mirror archival print publications.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric, editorial fluid grid balanced by generous whitespace intervals.

### Grid Rules
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `gutter-desktop` (2.5rem) and `margin-desktop` (4.5rem). Maximum layout container width is capped at 1440px to retain intimate proportions.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` (1.5rem) and `margin-tablet` (2.5rem).
- **Mobile (<767px):** 4-column layout with a 1rem gutter and `margin` (1.25rem).

### Vertical Rhythm
- Section spacing favors substantial negative space (`space-2xl`) between featured editorial modules, seasonal lookbooks, and product listing sets.
- Product listings alternate between standard two-to-four column grids and magazine-style staggered ratios (e.g., a 7-column feature portrait next to a 5-column double-stack card layout).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids simulated light sources, heavy dropshadows, and skeuomorphic blur gradients. Depth is established purely through surface-layer hierarchy, tonal contrast, and precise boundary lines.

### Elevation Principles
- **Base Tier (Ground):** Canvas ecru (`#F9F8F5`). All primary product photography sits flush with the base canvas.
- **Layer 1 (Card/Container Surfaces):** Soft limestone (`#F2EFEB`) or transparent panels resting on the base tier.
- **Hairline Framing:** Micro-borders (1px) rendered in `#E7E2DA` provide crisp division for sticky headers, category filters, and modal barriers without visual heaviness.
- **Overlays and Floating Sheets:** Sliding cart drawers, quick-view sheets, and mobile menus rely on solid ecru planes with an unhurried, linear opacity backdrop (`rgba(25, 24, 23, 0.35)`). A subtle ambient rim shadow (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(25, 24, 23, 0.08)`) is permitted solely on floating modals to differentiate them from the active canvas.

## Shapes

A strict `roundedness: 0` (sharp) geometry is enforced throughout this design system.

### Rationale & Rules
- Rectilinear, tailored precision mirrors the silhouette of high-end tailoring, bespoke stationery, and museum publication designs.
- All product card containers, interactive buttons, inputs, dropdown selectors, chips, and modals feature pure 90-degree corners (`0px` border-radius).
- Circular elements are strictly prohibited, with the sole exception of color swatch pickers and status dot pips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in primary charcoal (`#191817`), text in ecru (`#F9F8F5`), 0px radius, 52px height for desktop. Typography uses `label-uppercase`. Hover state transitions smoothly to raw umber (`#8C7A6B`).
- **Secondary (Outline):** 1px solid primary charcoal border, transparent background, primary charcoal typography. Hover state inverts background to primary charcoal with ecru text.
- **Tertiary (Text/Editorial Link):** Underlined text with a 1px offset baseline. On hover, the underline expands or transitions to raw umber.

### Product Badges & Chips
- **Badges:** Placed inside product image quadrants with an inset offset of 12px. Sharp rectilinear badges with an ecru ground (`#F9F8F5`), charcoal border (1px solid `#E7E2DA`), and `label-uppercase` typography (e.g., "LIMITED EDITION", "RAW SILK", "ARCHIVE").
- **Filter Chips:** Flat rectilinear pills with a transparent background and 1px border. When selected, the background turns primary charcoal with white/ecru text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Minimal bottom-border or four-sided subtle border (1px solid `#DCD6CD`) over a clean ecru background. Text inputs use `body-md` in charcoal. Focus state sharpens the border to 1px primary charcoal (`#191817`) without artificial outer glow halos.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unrounded, architectural checkboxes (square) and radio frames. Checked states feature solid charcoal fills.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Card:** Image container sits at a strict 3:4 aspect ratio with seamless, neutral-tinted backgrounds. Content details sit directly beneath the media container with zero padding, displaying title (`body-md`), fabric description (`body-sm` in raw umber), and price (`body-md` in charcoal).
- **Interactive Quick-Add:** Appears on image hover as a clean bottom bar within the 3:4 frame, maintaining the zero-radius aesthetic.

### Lists & Accordions
- Used for garment care instructions, sizing specifications, and origin documentation.
- Separated by horizontal 1px hairline rules (`#E7E2DA`). Accordion expanders use understated minimalist glyphs (`+` and `—`) set in Hanken Grotesk.
